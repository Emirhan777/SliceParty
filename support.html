SLICE PARTY

Help and support

Your phone is the sword. A computer or TV browser is the game screen. Slice Party is free, with no account or purchases required.

Start from the app

  1. Tap Create a room, then Create room link.
  2. Copy or share the link to your computer using Messages, email, AirDrop to a Mac, or another sharing app.
  3. Open it on the computer and select Open game on this screen.
  4. Return to the iPhone app. Your sword connects automatically.
  5. Choose Motion sword or Touch sword, then tap Start game.

Links work on one screen and expire after ten minutes. Create another if it expires. Returning from sharing does not cancel your link; leaving the setup page cancels an unused room.

Join an existing room

  1. Open emirhan777.github.io/SliceParty/ on a computer or compatible TV browser.
  2. In the app, tap Scan game QR, or type the six-digit room code and tap Join.
  3. Choose your controls and tap Start game. Two phones can join each room.

Both devices need internet, but they can use different Wi-Fi or mobile networks. Keep the big-screen page open. New room creates a fresh game room for that screen.

Swing and slice

Hold the phone securely, tilted back so you can see its display. Aim toward the middle of the screen and tap Center sword. Turn left and right or tilt up and down to move the sword. Use small, controlled motions with clear space around you. In touch mode, drag on the pad instead.

Slice fruit for points and combos. You have three lives; missing fruit costs a life and hitting a bomb ends the run. Tap Play again after a round.

Play on a TV

Use the TV's web browser, connect a computer by HDMI, or mirror a Mac to an AirPlay-compatible TV. Open the game on the computer or TV and keep your iPhone on the controller. Mirroring only the iPhone displays its controller interface, not a separate game screen.

Troubleshooting

  • Room closed: keep the big-screen browser open, then rejoin its current room.
  • Room full: leave from an unused phone or create another room. Each room supports two swords.
  • Camera denied: enter the code manually, or enable Camera for Slice Party in Settings.
  • Motion unavailable: use touch controls, or enable Motion access in Settings.
  • Sword pointing incorrectly: hold the phone tilted toward you and tap Center sword.
  • After locking the phone: return to the app and rejoin the room.
  • Connection problems: check both devices' internet and try a fresh room. The shared online service has capacity limits.

Contact

Report bugs or request help using the project's support issues. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and steps that caused the problem. Do not post private details or active room links.
