Slice Party is a native motion/touch controller for its companion browser game. No login or purchases are required. The iPhone is the controller; a separate computer or TV browser displays the game.

1. Open https://emirhan777.github.io/SliceParty/ on a computer and leave it open. A free room is created automatically.
2. On the iPhone, select Scan game QR and grant Camera permission, or type the six-digit room code and tap Join.
3. Select Motion sword and allow Motion access, or select Touch sword to test without sensors.
4. Tap Start game. Swing with small movements or drag on the touch pad. Watch the fruit and sword on the computer. Center sword resets the aim.
5. After a round, tap Play again. Up to two phones can join each room.

To test creating a room from the app, choose Create a room, then Create room link. Copy or share the link to a computer, open it there, and confirm Open game on this screen. Return to the app; it connects automatically. Links expire after ten minutes and work on one screen.

The camera only decodes QR codes locally. Raw motion sensor data stays on the phone; derived sword coordinates and game state use Firebase. There are no ads, analytics SDK, purchases, accounts, chat, or user-generated text. Privacy and support links are on the home screen. The phone and computer may use different internet connections. Native haptics and motion controls require a physical device; touch controls work in a simulator.
