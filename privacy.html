SLICE PARTY

Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026. Publisher: Emirhan ?im?ek.

Slice Party turns your iPhone into a sword controller for a game on a computer or TV. No account, name, email address, purchase, or subscription is required to play.

Camera and motion

The camera reads the game QR code only while the scanner is open. Camera images are processed on your device, are not saved or uploaded by Slice Party, and the app does not record audio. You can enter the room code without using the camera.

With motion controls enabled, phone orientation is converted into sword coordinates on your device. Those coordinates are sent to your game room while connected. Raw sensor readings stay on the device. Touch controls are available instead.

Game sessions and retention

Google Firebase Realtime Database relays room codes, random player identifiers, player slots, current sword coordinates, game commands, timestamps, scores, combos, and lives between devices. The latest movement replaces the previous movement; the game does not keep a movement history.

Player data is removed when you leave or disconnect. The room is removed when the big screen disconnects. Network failures can delay removal. Valid room codes allow anyone who knows the code to attempt to join, so share them only with intended players.

Sharing a room link

Creating a room link stores a random pairing token and an expiry time with the pending room. Links expire after ten minutes and can be claimed by one big screen. Cancelling the setup or the expiry timer removes unused reservations. If the app is terminated or offline before cleanup, an expired reservation may remain stored, but its link cannot start a game. After the screen claims the room, normal session cleanup applies.

The system share sheet sends a link through the service you choose. Copying a link puts it on your clipboard. These actions happen only when you choose them.

Service providers

Firebase receives technical connection information, including IP addresses, and may retain it for service operation and diagnostics under its own retention policies. This technical data may be linked to a device or network. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. See Firebase privacy and retention information.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose servers receive browser requests and IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement. The big-screen website requests a QR image from api.qrserver.com, sending the browser-controller URL and room code. This does not include camera images or the app's private pairing token. See the QR provider's privacy information.

On your device

The native app holds the current game session in memory. The browser game stores the best score locally in your browser; clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Advertising and analytics

Slice Party has no ads, in-app purchases, third-party advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDK. The Expo Go development preview is a separate developer app with its own policies; the standalone App Store app does not require Expo Go.

Your choices and contact

You can decline or revoke camera and motion permissions in iPhone Settings, enter a room code manually, use touch controls, or leave the room. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the publisher through Slice Party support. Issues are public: do not post personal information, active room links, or credentials. Describe the request without sensitive details so we can arrange an appropriate follow-up.
